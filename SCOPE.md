# Scope

## In scope

- World models: learned simulators of environments for prediction, planning, and decision-making.
- Papers, preprints, and surveys on world models (video, 3D/spatial, driving, robotics/embodied, game/interactive).
- Open-source implementations, frameworks, and pretrained checkpoints.
- Datasets and benchmarks used to train/evaluate world models.
- Tools, libraries, and simulators supporting world-model research.
- Tutorials, talks, and educational resources.

## Out of scope

- Generic video generators unrelated to world simulation.
- Pure perception models without predictive dynamics.
- Closed-source products without public resources.
- Unverified or low-quality links.

## Inclusion criteria

- Publicly accessible (paper page, code, or dataset).
- Relevant to world modeling.
- Clear title and one-sentence description.
- Prefer primary sources.
